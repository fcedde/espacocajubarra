Talk to the salon
Do I need an appointment?+

Yes, service is by appointment. You can use the online booking (AVEC), which shows available times right away, or message us on WhatsApp.

Can I do hair and nails on the same day?+

Yes. That’s the advantage of having everything under one roof — hair, nails, lashes, brows and waxing. Let us know when booking so we can schedule the services in sequence.

How long does it take?+

It depends on the service. Cuts and polish are usually quicker; extensions, color and treatments take longer. Tell us what you want when booking so we reserve the right amount of time.

Where is the salon?+

At Barra Prime: Av. Afonso Arinos de Melo Franco, 222, Bloco 2, Loja 102 — Barra da Tijuca, Rio de Janeiro/RJ, CEP 22631-455.

What are the opening hours?+

Tuesday to Friday, 10am–7pm. Saturday, 9am–6pm. Closed Monday and Sunday.
